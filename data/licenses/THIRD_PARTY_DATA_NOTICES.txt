MODERN WARS
Original game by man_82. Steam version developed by angel. Scenario contributors include UnionOfBritain, orange, filip, and Randombanana.

DAYTIME IMAGERY: NASA BLUE MARBLE NEXT GENERATION
NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble Next Generation. Imagery prepared and graded for Modern Wars. No NASA endorsement is implied.

NIGHT IMAGERY: NASA BLACK MARBLE 2016
NASA Earth Observatory images by Joshua Stevens, using Suomi NPP VIIRS data from Miguel Roman, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. Black Marble 2016 observations and 2019 color composites; locally reprojected and graded.

NATURAL EARTH GEOGRAPHIC REFERENCE
Natural Earth public-domain physical relief, administrative boundaries and rivers; prepared for Modern Wars.

MAIN ROADS: GRIP4
GRIP4, Meijer, Huijbregts, Schotten and Schipper (2018), Global patterns of current and future road infrastructure, Environmental Research Letters 13-064006. CC0; DOI: 10.1088/1748-9326/aabd42. Centerlines prepared for Modern Wars.

TERRAIN REFERENCE: RESOLVE ECOREGIONS 2017
RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017, Dinerstein et al. (2017), https://ecoregions.world/ . CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Modified by rasterization and a game terrain crosswalk.

ELEVATION REFERENCE: NOAA ETOPO 2022
NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information, ETOPO 2022, 60 arcsecond v1 surface elevation. CC0; DOI: 10.25921/fd45-gt74. Locally derived slope and relief.

NATIONAL AND ALLIANCE FLAGS
National and alliance flag artwork accompanies the supplied scenarios. Scenario-specific credits identify contributors. Player-imported flags are local user content.

BUILDINGS, AIRCRAFT, SHIPS, MISSILES AND COMBAT EFFECTS
Buildings and effects include artwork from the supplied OpenGameArt and other asset packs. Procedural vehicle silhouettes and era variants are created for Modern Wars.

AUDIO AND PLAYER-SELECTED MUSIC
Standard and nuclear-impact mixes created by the Modern Wars developer using free-to-use source sounds. Other supplied effects retain their individual source notices. Original reference soundtrack recordings are excluded from customer packages. Player-selected music is local playback only.
